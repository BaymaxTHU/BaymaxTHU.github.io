Laboratory Research

UDepthPD: Physics-Guided Underwater Monocular Depth Estimation

A latent diffusion framework with underwater imaging priors

DiffusionDepth EstimationStable Diffusion
  • Designed a three-channel IMT physical prior from grayscale intensity, GDCP transmission, and green/blue channel maxima to preserve structure and wavelength-dependent attenuation cues.
  • Built on Marigold and Stable Diffusion v2.1, concatenating RGB, depth, and prior representations in latent space and fine-tuning the U-Net for one-step inference.
  • Reconstructed high-fidelity depth labels for USOD10K; outperformed baselines on seven metrics (AbsRel 0.1642, δ₁ 0.8962) and demonstrated zero-shot generalization.

Undergraduate Thesis

Unified Multimodal Visual Perception

Dual-granularity representations with task-agnostic decoding

MultimodalTransformerMulti-task Learning
  • Encoded images into global and regional proposal tokens and used a shared task-agnostic Transformer decoder for classification, image-text retrieval, and object detection.
  • Unified classes, retrieval samples, and detection regions as candidate sets optimized through input-candidate similarity and maximum likelihood.
  • Applied gradient normalization and sampling-ratio compensation to improve stability in joint training with large-batch tasks.

AI AgentEvidence GraphResearch Tools
